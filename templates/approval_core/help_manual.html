End User (Submitter) Guide

The End User is the starting point of the SMVS Approval System workflow. Your primary role is to initiate requests and provide all necessary documentation for verification.

📂 End User Workspace & Features
Dashboard

Displays a high-level summary of "Forms Pending," "Approved," and "Rejected" specifically for your submissions.

Revision Management

A dedicated section highlights forms sent back for revision with high-contrast remarks so you know exactly what to fix.

Search & History

Search through past requests using filters to find specific forms, subjects, status or amounts.

🕒 Workflow Interaction
  • Submission: If you are in a foreign country, the system automatically detects your local currency and converts it to INR for the audit trail.
  • Document Upload: Upload evidence as a PDF. You can add documents until the form is officially submitted into the workflow.
  • Real-time Tracking: Use the Approval Flow tab to see which office (e.g., "MK Sabhya") currently holds your form.
  • Comment Visibility: View feedback only when approvers check the "Show to Lower Levels" option.
